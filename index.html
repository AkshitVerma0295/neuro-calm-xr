Clinical Data

Summary of fictional phase 3 results.

NeuroCalm XR is indicated for the management of moderate neuropathic pain in adults when non-pharmacologic measures and standard analgesics have provided insufficient relief.

  • Adults 18 years and older
  • Not studied in pediatric patients
  • Not indicated for acute pain

In a fictional 12-week, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (n = 842), patients receiving NeuroCalm XR reported a mean reduction in pain intensity score of 2.4 points versus 1.1 points with placebo (p < 0.001).

Secondary endpoints showed improvements in sleep quality and daily function. These figures are illustrative only.

The most common adverse reactions (≥5% and greater than placebo) in fictional trials were:

  • Dizziness (9%)
  • Somnolence (7%)
  • Nausea (6%)
  • Peripheral edema (5%)

Safety Information

Important Safety Information (Fictional)

Contraindications: Known hypersensitivity to NeuroCalm XR or any of its excipients.

Warnings and Precautions: May cause dizziness and somnolence; advise patients not to drive until they know how the drug affects them. Use with caution in patients with hepatic impairment. Monitor for peripheral edema.
